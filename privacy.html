Privacy

Updated November 26, 2026

What we measure

To learn which parts of this page are useful, the site records how each visit goes:

  • where the visit came from (the referring site or campaign link),
  • device type, browser, operating system, screen size and language,
  • approximate location (city and country), worked out by our host from the connection,
  • how far you scroll, how long each section is on screen, what you click, which photos you open and how much of the video you watch,
  • whether the booking calendar was opened.

A random ID in your browser's storage lets us tell a return visit from a first one.

What we don't collect

  • No names, emails or anything you type.
  • No IP addresses are stored.
  • Nothing from inside the booking calendar. Bookings are handled by our booking provider under its own privacy policy.
  • No advertising trackers, no third-party analytics, and the data is never sold or shared.

How long we keep it

Visit records are deleted automatically after 90 days.

Opting out

If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal, nothing is recorded. You can also clear this site's storage in your browser settings to reset the random ID.

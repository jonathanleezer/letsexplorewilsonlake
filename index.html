Cabin 102 · The Cove at Doublehead

A warm home base
right on the water.

This isn't a hotel room with a lake nearby. It's a lakefront home where the evenings are the main event.

When the air turns cool, the wood-burning fireplace becomes the heart of the cabin. Large west-facing windows frame Wilson Lake all day, so you can watch the early sunset from inside, then bundle up on the oversized porch swing or walk down to the private dock as the last light fades over the water.

The fully equipped kitchen is made for slow suppers and soup-on-the-stove nights—and Walmart+ and Sam's Club can deliver groceries and household essentials directly to the front door, so once you settle in, you never have to leave.

More than a cabin

Room to wander.
Room to be quiet.

The Cove at Doublehead is a waterfront community on Wilson Lake. In the cooler months it's made for long walks along the shore and a little fishing off the dock, with the pool, playground and courts there for warmer visits. Everyone meets back at the cabin for dinner and sunset.

Stay in or venture out

The lake is quieter
this time of year.

Coffee on the dock in a warm sweater. A slow walk along the shore. A few hours of fishing. Soup on the stove, a book by the fire, and the last light crossing still water before dinner.

When you want a change of scenery, the cabin is your launch point for Florence, Muscle Shoals, Sheffield and Tuscumbia—four towns packed with music history, architecture, food and Tennessee River character.
